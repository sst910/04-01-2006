Didi… ❤️

Mujhe nahi pata ki aapke mann mein mere liye kya chal raha hai ya aapne mere baare mein kya soch liya hai, lekin aapka mujhse is tarah khamosh rehna mujhe andar se bahut takleef de raha hai.

Mere saath jo hua, jis insaan par maine bharosa kiya aur jisne mera bharosa tod diya, uska dard main kisi tarah sambhaal lunga. Lekin Didi, jab mujhe sabse zyada apne kisi ka saath chahiye, tab aapse hi apne dil ki baat nahi keh paa raha hoon. Aur sach kahun, toh ye baat mujhe aur zyada tod rahi hai.

Main maanta hoon ki mujhse galtiyan hui hongi. Main ye nahi keh raha ki main bilkul sahi hoon ya aap mujhe bina kuch poochhe sahi maan lo. Bas itna chahta hoon ki aap ek baar meri baat bhi poori sun lo. Maine shuru mein bhi aapko samjhaane ki koshish ki thi aur kaha tha ki engagement se aane ke baad sab kuch sach-sach bataunga. Shayad main apni baat theek se samjha nahi paaya, lekin main chahta hoon ki aap meri taraf ki baat bhi jaano.

Didi, main duniya ke saamne khud ko kitna bhi strong dikha loon, kitna bhi keh doon ki main theek hoon, lekin hoon toh aapse chhota hi na. Aapke saamne toh main bina strong bane, bina apna dard chhupaaye, apne mann ki baat kehna chahta hoon. Har kisi ke saamne main apni feelings nahi bata sakta, lekin aapse toh keh sakta hoon na?

Mujhe pata hai ki aapki zindagi mein bhi bahut bada badlaav aane wala hai aur aapke mann mein bahut kuch chal raha hoga. Main aapki pareshani samajhta hoon aur kabhi nahi chahta ki meri wajah se aap aur pareshaan ho. Bas is waqt mujhe bhi apni Didi ke saath ki zaroorat hai.

Aap mujhe daant lena, meri galtiyaan bata dena, mujhse sawaal kar lena. Main sab sununga. Bas meri baat sune bina mujhse door mat ho jaana, Didi. Aapki naraazgi se zyada takleef aapki khamoshi deti hai, kyunki samajh nahi aata ki aapse apna dard kaise kahun.

Main aapse ye nahi chahta ki aap mujhe har baat mein sahi maano. Main bas chahta hoon ki ek baar meri jagah khud ko rakhkar samajhne ki koshish karo ki main kis takleef se guzar raha hoon. Main pehle se hi khud ko sambhaalne ki koshish kar raha hoon, bas is waqt mujhe ye mehsoos hona chahiye ki meri Didi mere liye abhi bhi hai, jiske saamne main apna dard keh sakta hoon.

Didi, main aapse apni galti chhupaane ya khud ko sahi saabit karne nahi aaya. Main bas chahta hoon ki aap meri baat suno, mera dard samjho aur mujhe apni baat kehne ka ek mauka do.

Please Didi, ek baar mujhse baat kar lo. Main aapko sab kuch bataana chahta hoon, bina kuch chhupaaye. Aap mujhe samjha dena ki main kahan galat hoon, bas mujhe itna akela mat mehsoos hone dena ki main apni Didi se bhi apne dil ki baat na keh paaun.

Aapka chhota bhai hoon, Didi…
mujhe bhi kabhi-kabhi aapke saath
aur samajh ki zaroorat hoti hai. ❤️

Dil se likha hai — bina kisi shikayat ke, bas apnapan ke saath.

ki hum phir se khulkar baat kar sakein. 🫂
